My Grandmother's Sunday Sauce

Every Sunday the whole house smelled of garlic and basil. Here is the story of the sauce, with the recipe woven into the prose the way she taught me — a little of this, a handful of that. There is no neat ingredient list here on purpose.

You start with olive oil and a few cloves of garlic, then a couple cans of San Marzano tomatoes crushed by hand, a generous pinch of salt, and basil at the end.
