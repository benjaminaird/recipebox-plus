Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Makes about 24 cookies · Bake 350°F for 11 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1 cup raisins

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line two baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Cream the butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Whisk together flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt; stir into the butter mixture.
  5. Fold in the oats and raisins until evenly combined.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons 2 inches apart and bake 11 minutes until golden at the edges.
  7. Cool on the sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
