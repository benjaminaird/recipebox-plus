Delete Your Hearthkeep Account

You can delete your Hearthkeep account directly inside the app.

In-App Steps

Open Hearthkeep, go to Settings, find Danger Zone, type DELETE, enter your password, and choose Delete my account.

What Is Deleted

Hearthkeep deletes your cloud account, saved recipes, meal plan, account sessions, password reset tokens, and app settings associated with your account.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account, email hello@recipeboxapp.com from the email address on your Hearthkeep account.
