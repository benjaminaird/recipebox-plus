Need help with Hearthkeep? Email hello@recipeboxapp.com.

Common Help Topics

Password reset: Use the reset link on the sign-in screen. Reset links expire for security.

Recipe import: Public links, YouTube videos, captions, photos, screenshots, and PDFs work best when the recipe text is visible or spoken clearly.

AI limits: Beta accounts have a monthly AI request limit to keep costs predictable while Hearthkeep grows.

Backups: Open Settings to export a local backup of your recipes and meal plan.
