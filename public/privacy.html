Hearthkeep Privacy Policy

Effective June 17, 2026

Hearthkeep helps you save, import, organize, and cook from your recipes. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Hearthkeep may collect account information such as your email address, display name, password hash, saved recipes, meal plans, timer preferences, import history needed to run the app, and AI usage counts.

If you upload photos, PDFs, screenshots, links, captions, or handwritten recipe images, Hearthkeep processes that content to extract or clean up recipes.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide account sign-in, recipe storage and sync, recipe imports, AI recipe cleanup, Pantry Chef suggestions, meal planning, support, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

AI Processing

Hearthkeep sends recipe text, captions, uploaded images, PDFs, or related prompts to AI providers only when you use AI-powered features. Do not upload content you do not want processed for recipe extraction or recipe assistance.

Data Sharing

Hearthkeep uses service providers for hosting, database storage, email delivery, and AI processing. These providers process information only as needed to operate Hearthkeep. Hearthkeep does not sell your personal information.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account in Hearthkeep from Settings. Deleting your account removes your cloud account, recipes, meal plan, sessions, reset tokens, and related settings. See Delete Account for details.

Children

Hearthkeep is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to Hearthkeep, contact us and we will help remove it.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@recipeboxapp.com.
