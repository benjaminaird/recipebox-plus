Appearance setting

System is the default. It follows the iPhone's Light, Dark or Automatic setting and switches live, including at sunset, without a relaunch.

Light and Dark pin the app regardless of the phone. The choice is saved on the device and restored on launch.

This prototype behaves the same way. The Appearance control above is the in-app setting (also in the app under the avatar on Home). It is remembered in this browser. In System it follows your viewer's theme and updates when that changes.

Native mapping

  • iOS: an asset-catalog color per token with Any and Dark values; overrideUserInterfaceStyle for Light or Dark; .unspecified for System.
  • Android: values and values-night resources; AppCompatDelegate.setDefaultNightMode with FOLLOW_SYSTEM, NO or YES.

Semantic tokens, both appearances

Read live from the prototype's stylesheet. Contrast is calculated against the surface each text token actually sits on. WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and controls.

Text size: 100%, 150%, 200%

100%Two-column recipe grid. Step portions sit in the margin beside each step. Four facts in one row. 150%The grid becomes a single-column list with large thumbnails. Margin portions move under the step as labelled chips. Facts wrap to two rows. Ingredient names drop below their quantity. 200%The same rules as 150%. Cook Mode stacks Next above Back, and step text reaches about 50pt. Tab bar labels stay at their standard size, as on iOS. A long-press would show the enlarged label in a native build.

Nothing truncates at any scale. Titles wrap, and the inline navigation title is the only text that shortens with an ellipsis, as on iOS.

Accessibility checks

  • Contrast: every text token passes AA in both appearances (see table). Secondary and tertiary text stay above 4.5:1.
  • Tap targets: every control is at least 44 × 44 pt. Turn on Tap targets above to outline them. Anything smaller is drawn in red and counted under the phone.
  • VoiceOver and TalkBack: real headings, a named control for every icon button, a radio group for Appearance, and ingredient rows read as quantity then name. Timer completion is announced in a live region.
  • Cooking: step text is 25pt serif at 100% with 1.42 line height. The step's own portions are listed right under it. The screen stays awake.
  • Status never uses colour alone. Every flag carries an icon and a word.
  • Reduced motion: sheet and screen transitions are turned off.

Decisions for your review

  • Serif in Cook Mode. Newsreader at 25pt reads like a printed cookbook. The alternative is SF Pro at the same size, which is slightly more legible from across a counter.
  • Dark accent. Sage green (#9FCDB0) for text and icons on a deeper green button. Is this the right partner for the light botanical green?
  • Photo dimming in Dark. Photos are dimmed about 12% so they don't glare at night. Undimmed is an option.
  • Recipes without photos get a quiet typographic tile instead of a stock image.

After you approve, I'll finalize the token values and record the visual direction as an ADR. No native code has been started.
